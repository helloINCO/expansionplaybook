We’re a design studio, not a data company. Here is what happens to the details you give us.

INCO Studio Pty Ltd (“INCO”, “we”, “us”) is a hospitality and retail design studio in Sydney. This policy covers the personal information we handle through the playbook page at playbook.incostudio.co, and any other INCO page that links to it. We aim to handle that information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

The short version

  • We ask for your name, email, phone number and venue or company so we can send you the playbook and follow up.
  • If you take the readiness check, your four answers are saved against that record so our follow-up fits where you are.
  • Cookies and ad tools tell us how the page performs and which ads brought you here.
  • We don’t sell your information. The few tools that store or process it are named in section 3.
  • You can ask to see it, fix it, delete it, or stop hearing from us, at any time.

1. What we collect

It depends on what you do on the page.

What you give us

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and the venue or company you operate
  • Your answers to the four readiness questions, the score they produce, and the area the check flags as your weakest
  • If you book a call, the details you enter in the booking calendar, including anything you write there
  • Anything you tell us when you email or speak to us

What we collect automatically

  • Your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you view
  • How you got here, including campaign tags in the link (UTM tags, and click IDs such as Facebook’s fbclid)
  • The time you submitted the form and the page you submitted it from
  • Cookie and pixel identifiers set by the tools in section 5

We don’t ask for payment details, government ID numbers or sensitive information such as health, race, religion or sexual orientation. Please don’t send them to us.

If you arrive through an ad, the ad platform tells us which campaign you came from. We don’t buy contact lists or add details to your record from other sources.

2. Why we collect it and how we use it

We use your information to:

  • send you The Expansion Playbook by email
  • respond to your messages, and set up and run any call you book
  • follow up by email or phone about the playbook, your answers, and how INCO could help
  • tailor that follow-up, because your readiness result decides which emails you receive and whether we suggest a call
  • measure the page and our advertising, and show INCO ads to people who have visited it
  • keep the page secure, block spam, and meet our legal obligations

When you submit the form, you agree to hear from us about the playbook and our services. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and we act on it promptly and always within five business days. We won’t use your information for anything unrelated to the above without asking you first, unless the law requires it.

We don’t sell personal information.

3. Who we share it with

These are the providers that handle your information, and they handle it only for the purposes in section 2.

HighLevel
Also branded GoHighLevel and LeadConnector. It is our CRM. It receives what you submit in the form, your readiness result and campaign tags, sends our emails, and runs the booking calendar.
Google
Google Tag Manager loads our measurement tools. Google Analytics and Google Ads, where active, receive page activity and cookie identifiers.
Meta
Where the Meta Pixel is active, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) receives page activity, form-submission events and cookie identifiers, so we can measure and target our ads.
Hosting and delivery
Our web host and content delivery providers, such as jsDelivr, receive your IP address when the page loads.
Our team and advisers
Our team (some of whom work remotely), contractors, and professional advisers such as lawyers and accountants, where they need it to do their job.

Google and Meta handle the data they receive under their own privacy policies. Beyond the settings we choose, we can’t see or control what they do with it.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety, or as part of a sale or restructure of our business. The page links to LinkedIn and Instagram, which have their own policies.

4. Sending information overseas

Several of the providers above store and process data outside Australia, mainly in the United States. We choose established providers and take reasonable steps to make sure they handle your information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. We can’t promise another country’s laws will protect it in the same way.

5. Cookies and tracking

Cookies and pixels are small files and snippets of code that recognise your browser. We use them to keep the page working, to measure how it is used, and to measure and target our advertising.

You can control them:

  • Set your browser to block or delete cookies. The form still works without them.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on.
  • Adjust Google ads in your Google ad settings.
  • Adjust Meta ads in your ad preferences.
  • Opt out of many ad networks at optout.networkadvertising.org.

We don’t respond to Do Not Track signals, because there is no agreed standard for them yet. The options above are the reliable route.

6. How long we keep it

If you don’t become a client, we keep your details for up to 24 months after our last contact, then delete or de-identify them.

If you unsubscribe, we keep your email address on a suppression list so we don’t contact you again. Cookie and analytics data is kept according to the retention settings in our Google and Meta accounts.

7. Keeping it safe

Access to your details is limited to the team members who need it, and the systems that hold them sit behind password-protected accounts. The form has spam protection built in.

No system is perfectly secure. If something goes wrong that is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

8. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong or out of date
  • delete your information
  • stop contacting you, or stop using your information for marketing

Email hello@incostudio.co. We’ll reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm it’s you first, and in limited cases the law lets us decline a request, in which case we’ll tell you why.

To stop our marketing emails, use the unsubscribe link in any of them. To limit ad tracking, use the options in section 5.

9. Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your information, email us at hello@incostudio.co with the details and we’ll reply within 30 days.

If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

10. Under 18s

This page is written for business owners and operators. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think we have, email us and we’ll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We’ll update this page when our practices change and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll email the people on our mailing list.
